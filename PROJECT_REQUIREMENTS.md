# Soniaa portfolio requirements

## Purpose and audience

Bring Soniaa’s leadership as a fractional CMO to life on a single responsive page. Help potential clients and partners, hiring teams, event organizers and professional peers find a relevant story and start a conversation.

## Content and design decisions

- Use the LinkedIn profile at `linkedin.com/in/soniaspecialist/` and the profile screenshots supplied by the client for biographical facts. The user’s SlideShare and YouTube links are the featured media. Preserve the public name “Soniaa” and the spelling used within attributed quotations.
- The selected design is **Midnight Stage**. It uses a dark stage, warm amber light and ivory typography. Use the reference sites for interaction ideas only. Do not source biography from Niswey.
- Keep the visitor experience on one page. Hash links can open topics directly. The private owner report has its own route.
- Keep story claims grounded in the supplied experience. Recommendations stay attributed. Expired credentials retain their dates.

## Acceptance criteria

| Requirement | A visitor or owner can verify it by |
| --- | --- |
| Full-stack capabilities | Submitting a valid enquiry and finding its subject, reason and message in the private inbox |
| Interactions and animation | Switching among brand, demand and leadership stories, opening details and sharing a story link |
| Responsive access | Navigating by keyboard, expanding a topic on a narrow screen and using the contact form with reduced motion enabled |
| Functional contact | Starting an enquiry from a work story with the subject preselected or opening Soniaa’s LinkedIn profile |
| Meaningful engagement | Comparing recorded visits, story views, form starts and submitted messages by topic and purpose |
| Private testing | Keeping the production inbox address and server credentials out of client assets, and isolating local test data |
| Launch readiness | Confirming delivery to the approved destination and obtaining authorization before public launch |

The approved first cloud test recipient is Vivek’s personal inbox. Configure that address on the server. The downloadable build stores local test enquiries on the developer’s computer.
